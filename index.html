Kirstin Stevens

Author, poet and systems strategist for the relational age of intelligence.

Kirstin works at the intersection of AI, education and neurodiversity. She is the founder of The Novacene, the studio behind Verse-ality — a framework for agent safety and relational intelligence — and she designs online and hybrid schools and alternative provision, advising institutions on AI governance and safeguarding.
